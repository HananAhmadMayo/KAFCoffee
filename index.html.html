Our Story

Crafted with Passion, Served with Pride

Nestled in the heart of Islamabad's F-6 Markaz, KAF Coffee has become the city's most beloved specialty coffee destination. From our signature Flat White to our legendary Tiramisu, every item on our menu is crafted with meticulous attention to detail.

Our space balances modern elegance with cozy warmth — whether you're seeking a quiet corner for focused work, a romantic evening spot, or a vibrant gathering place with friends.
